SUBPROFILE EXPLORER


This tool provides access to the bioactivity cluster similarity for 1,883 reference (landmark) compounds as determined using subprofiles derived from the Cell painting analysis in U2OS cells upon compound treatment for 20 h. Thus far, thirteen clusters were defined that are related to modulation of AKT/PI3K/MTOR, Aurora kinases, BET, DNA synthesis, HDAC, HSP90, Na+ /K+ATPase and tubulin or processes like lysosomotropism/cholesterol homeostasis regulation (L/CH), mitochondrial stress (MitoStress), protein synthesis, pyrimidine synthesis and uncoupling of the mitochondrial proton gradient.

Modulation of targets that does not lead to CPA profiles upon perturbation of U2OS cells with small molecules: aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AhR), indoleamine 2,3-dioxygenase (IDO1)
